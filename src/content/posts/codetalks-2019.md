<p>Last week I&#8217;ve had the honor and pleasure to speak at <a href="https://www.codetalks.de/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener" aria-label=" (opens in a new tab)">CodeTalks</a> 2019 @Hamburg.</p>



<p>All in all it was a great experience. The organizing team was very helpful and the event itself was organized amazingly (especially considering there were about 2000 people in the conference).</p>



<p>I also got to hang out with Yonatan and Liat Doron and Gal Maimon from <a href="https://hodash.dev/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener" aria-label=" (opens in a new tab)">HodashDev</a>.</p>



<p>Since I had two tickets, courtesy of the conference organizers for speakers, I&#8217;ve invited  Piotr and Kamil from <a href="https://warsawjs.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener" aria-label=" (opens in a new tab)">WarsawJS</a>. It was great hanging out with these guys.</p>



<figure class="wp-block-image size-large"><img data-recalc-dims="1" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="640" height="480" src="/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/HangingOutInHamburg.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-18" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/HangingOutInHamburg.jpg 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/12/HangingOutInHamburg.jpg 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/12/HangingOutInHamburg.jpg 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/12/HangingOutInHamburg.jpg 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/12/HangingOutInHamburg.jpg 1600w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/12/HangingOutInHamburg.jpg 1280w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 640px) 100vw, 640px" /><figcaption>Hanging out with awesome people &#8211; from bottom right: Liat Doron, Yonatan Doron, Kamil  Dzieniszewski, me, Piotr  Zientara and Gal Maimon</figcaption></figure>



<p>The first talk I&#8217;ve been to was <a href="https://www.codetalks.de/program#talk-674?event=5" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener" aria-label=" (opens in a new tab)">Time Driven Development</a> by Katerina Trajchevska. This talk was the best definition for a software architect I&#8217;ve ever heard.  You really should go ahead and watch it.</p>



<p>Yonatan Doron&#8217;s talk about micro interactions was a refreshing breeze &#8211; design importance in the life of programmers.  You should definitely <a href="https://www.codetalks.de/program#talk-937?event=5" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener" aria-label=" (opens in a new tab)">check it out too</a>.</p>



<p>I went to various talks &#8211; most of which were really great. I wish I could go to all&#8230; luckily, all of the talks are online on YouTube!</p>



<p>You can also check my talk about <a href="https://youtu.be/gBoBUSB-sNU" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener" aria-label=" (opens in a new tab)">optimization design patterns in web development</a>.</p>



<p>In summery &#8211; CodeTalks 2019 was an amazing experience.  I really hope I can get back to Hamburg for CodeTalks 2020.<br></p>
