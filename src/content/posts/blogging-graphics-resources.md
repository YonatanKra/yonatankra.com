<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Why_not_to_use_Google_Image_Search"></span>Why not to  use Google Image Search<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p>If you fear copyright, I suggest not to use google image search. The problem with it is that most pictures are copyrighted.  </p>



<p>Having said that, there&#8217;s an option to search for &#8220;free to use&#8221; images:</p>



<div class="wp-block-image"><figure class="aligncenter size-large"><img data-recalc-dims="1" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="640" height="179" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-3.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1212" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-3.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-3.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-3.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-3.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-3.png 268w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-3.png 1660w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-3.png 1280w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 640px) 100vw, 640px" /><figcaption>Google images. Use the tools (arrow 1) and then select Usage Rights (arrow 2).</figcaption></figure></div>



<p>Then again, it is stated that this feature is not bullet proof:</p>



<div class="wp-block-image"><figure class="aligncenter size-large"><img data-recalc-dims="1" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="640" height="450" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-4.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1213" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-4.png 1024w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-4.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-4.png 768w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-4.png 1536w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-4.png 128w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-4.png 1554w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/image-4.png 1280w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 640px) 100vw, 640px" /><figcaption>Surrounded with a red frame are the small prints.  While you searched for a &#8220;free to use&#8221; image, you can&#8217;t fully trust the result to be &#8220;free to use&#8221;.</figcaption></figure></div>



<p>So&#8230; let&#8217;s look for other alternatives that are 100% legally free.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Free_Images"></span>Free Images<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p>You should be aware of copyright policies.  I had 2 friends who got a letter from a lawyer claiming they are illegally using copyrighted images. Here are great sources for free images:</p>



<p><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://pixabay.com/" data-type="URL" data-id="https://pixabay.com/" target="_blank">Pixabay</a> &#8211; a really nice website. If you are also into gaming, it has nice audio section where you can download free audio files as well as images.</p>



<p><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://unsplash.com/" data-type="URL" data-id="https://unsplash.com/" target="_blank">Unsplash</a> &#8211; images for free 🙂</p>

<p> </p>

<p>There comes a time when you want to merge images together. You can throw them inside a google drawings canvas and mix them up.  Sometimes, it is useful to blur their edges so they will blend in an environment.</p>



<p>Here&#8217;s something I did with it:</p>



<div class="wp-block-image"><figure class="aligncenter size-full"><img data-recalc-dims="1" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="640" height="272" src="/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Blur-Edges.png" alt="" class="wp-image-1211" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Blur-Edges.png 666w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Blur-Edges.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2021/12/Blur-Edges.png 212w" sizes="auto, (max-width: 640px) 100vw, 640px" /><figcaption>Blurring the edges can be very useful to compose several images with a background</figcaption></figure></div>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Image_Editing_with_Google_Drawings"></span>Image Editing with Google Drawings<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p>This is a simple, yet powerful and free image editing option.  You can copy images, add all sorts of annotations and download your image to your computer.  Very easy to use.</p>



<p><a href="https://docs.google.com/drawings" data-type="URL" data-id="https://docs.google.com/drawings" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Check it out</a></p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading"><span class="ez-toc-section" id="Image_Editing_with_Canva"></span>Image Editing with Canva<span class="ez-toc-section-end"></span></h2>



<p>Canva is a nice image editor. It has lots of free images to use and nice Photoshop-like effects.  You can use it for free. I never found the need to upgrade myself.</p>



<p><a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://bit.ly/canva-image-editor" data-type="URL" data-id="https://bit.ly/canva-image-editor" target="_blank">Check it out</a></p>
