<p>About a year and a half ago I spoke at fullstackon 2018 London. I just got off stage, and the first people to catch me were a bunch of guys who called themselves  <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://warsawjs.com/" target="_blank">WarsawJS</a>.</p>



<p>Still light headed from my talk, I let them convince me to solve some JS riddle.</p>



<p>I don&#8217;t really remember what was the riddle &#8211; I mainly remember the enthusiasm and energies flowing from these people.</p>



<p>Fast forward a year and half &#8211; it&#8217;s my second year in a row vising Warsaw for <a rel="noreferrer noopener" aria-label="ConfrontJS (opens in a new tab)" href="https://2019.confrontjs.com/" target="_blank">ConfrontJS</a>. This time, I came to lead a performance workshop as well go on stage for a lightning talk.</p>

Big success 🙂</figcaption></figure></div>



<p>The experience, just like last year, was energizing.  From what I see (and  I&#8217;ve been following <a rel="noreferrer noopener" aria-label="WarsawJS (opens in a new tab)" href="https://warsawjs.com/" target="_blank">WarsawJS</a> since July 2018),  <a rel="noreferrer noopener" href="https://warsawjs.com/" target="_blank">WarsawJS</a> combines enthusiastic leadership, highly motivated community and a lot of talent.</p>
